## Interview prep

- **Fine-tuning LLMs (be ready, it's the core):** SFT with PEFT/LoRA on a chat model (Hugging Face TRL `SFTTrainer`), what DPO does (learns from chosen vs rejected answers without a reward model), and what RLHF adds (a reward model plus RL).
- **Arabic data:** tokeniser issues with Arabic, diacritics, mixing Arabic and maths notation, and Arabic-first models (Jais, ALLaM, AceGPT).
- **Evaluation:** building an Arabic STEM benchmark (curriculum-aligned questions, rubrics, a step-by-step correctness check, human review by teachers), plus safety guardrails for students (refusals, no answers-only cheating mode).
- **Serving:** why vLLM is used (batching, paged attention) and latency targets for a classroom chat.
- **Ask them:** the team at KAUST, contract length, and which base model they plan to use.
