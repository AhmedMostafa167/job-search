## Interview prep

- **"Explain RAG to an airline operations manager."** Practise a 60-second, no-jargon version.
- **"How would you check an AI assistant is reliable before go-live?"**
  - Build a labelled test set.
  - Use task-level checks with several runs per task.
  - Trace every call.
  - Run a grounding check, like your `prices_grounded` check that passed 25 of 25 runs.
  - Monitor in production.
- **"GDPR / EU AI Act?"**
  - Know the four risk tiers.
  - Know the transparency duty for chatbots.
  - Know data minimisation, which is why your memory stores only typed facts, with a limit of 50 per user.
- **"Cloud?"** Be honest: Docker and CI so far, with a cloud course in progress.
- **Ask them:**
  - Which aviation problems is the GenAI team working on (operations, crew, customer service)?
  - Do they support the visa and relocation for this role?
