## Interview prep

- **Evaluation plan (they want one page first):**
  1. Goal and metrics: field-level exact match, numeric tolerance, and precision/recall per field.
  2. Sampling of pages, including Eastern Arabic numerals (٠١٢٣…) and mixed scripts.
  3. Error categories.
  4. Cost per statement per model.
  5. How results are rerun.
- **Arabic numerals:** normalise ٠–٩ (and Persian ۰–۹) to 0–9; watch for thousands and decimal separators (٬ and ٫) and right-to-left order in tables.
- **Sentiment baseline:** TF-IDF plus logistic regression first, then an Arabic BERT (CAMeLBERT or AraBERT); label a small set with clear guidelines; check agreement between labellers.
- **Reproducibility:** fixed seeds, pinned data versions (DVC), and notebooks that run top to bottom.
