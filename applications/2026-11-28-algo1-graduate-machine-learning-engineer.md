> About Us
> The Role
> You’ll join our B2C pod, the team building our shopper experience, and help build the models at the heart of how our product understands shoppers. You’ll work directly with a product leader, translating product priorities into technical solutions.
> The ML problems here are unusually hard and unusually human. Shoppers make sequential decisions across millions of baskets and tens of thousands of products, and their preferences shift with the week, the season, the budget and the household. Modelling that well means designing graphs that capture how products and people really relate, and building transformer, graph and reinforcement learning models that learn from them. Grocery is what makes this so rich: weekly routines and impulse buys, family meals and Friday treats, swaps, habits and the occasional change of heart in the aisle. You’ll build a deep understanding of grocery behaviour and use it to develop better models.
> You’ll learn from a team that combines world-class research with real-world impact. Between them, they’ve published in Nature Machine Intelligence and with Google DeepMind, built recommender systems serving a million requests a day and scaled reinforcement learning at one of Europe’s leading AI labs. You’ll have the opportunity to get models into the hands of real shoppers within weeks. We’re agentic-first and everyone has access to max-tier AI tools, so you’ll never be rate-limited.
> What You’ll Work On
> Modelling how people shop. Build graphs of shoppers, products, missions and occasions that capture substitutes, complements and household needs.
> State-of-the-art models. Apply and extend graph neural networks, sequence models and reinforcement learning, judging success by how well they solve real shopper problems.
> Real-time personalisation. Respond to a shopper’s context in the moment, in a physical store, where timing matters as much as accuracy.
> Experiments that matter. Design A/B tests that measure real impact on shoppers and shape what the pod builds next.
> Production. Take models from research to live product with engineering, and own them once they’re out in the world.
