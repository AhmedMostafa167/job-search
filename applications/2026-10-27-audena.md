## Interview prep

- **Database depth:** reading an EXPLAIN plan, when an index isn't used, pgvector HNSW vs IVFFlat, combining full-text and vector results (Reciprocal Rank Fusion), and Alembic migrations without downtime.
- **LLM reliability:** retries with backoff, fallbacks to another provider (your provider factory in the Research Assistant), validating structured output, and timeouts.
- **Async and real time (gap):** async vs threads in Python, why a voice pipeline needs streaming (latency budget under 800 ms), and what a WebSocket is.
- **Multi-tenancy:** tenant isolation in PostgreSQL (a tenant_id on every row, or row-level security), and rate limiting.
- **Ask them:** company website and founders, the team size, contract type and pay currency.
