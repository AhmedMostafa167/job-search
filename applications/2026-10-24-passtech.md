## Interview prep

- **Memory design (they'll dig here):**
  - What you store: typed facts, not raw chat.
  - How facts are extracted and when they're written.
  - Conflicts and updates ("I'm vegetarian now").
  - Recall at the start of a conversation.
  - The cap of 50 facts per user.
  - Deleting a user's data.
  - Guardrails on memory writes (0 of 21 legitimate facts refused).
- **LangMem (gap):** LangChain's long-term memory library. It extracts memories from conversations, stores them and manages them in the background. Compare it with your typed-facts design.
- **Qdrant vs pgvector:** a dedicated vector database vs vectors alongside your relational data; filtering; HNSW settings.
- **Streaming (gap):** SSE from FastAPI (`StreamingResponse`, token streaming) vs WebSockets.
- **OpenRouter:** one API in front of many models, like the provider factory in your Research Assistant.
- **Reliability:** structured outputs, retries, guardrails, evaluation before shipping a prompt change.
- **Arabic:** "Sanad" suggests Arabic-speaking users, so mention that you're a native speaker.
