## Interview prep

- **A booking chatbot for Masaj (likely case):**
  - Flow: intent → slots (service, date and time, area, therapist gender) → availability tool → confirm → book. Hand off to a human for complaints.
  - Arabic and English, including Kuwaiti dialect.
  - Use RAG over the service catalogue and FAQ (prices, durations, areas).
- **Cost and speed:** cache common answers, use smaller or faster models for routing, cap the context, stream replies, and measure the cost per conversation.
- **Accuracy:** ground prices in data, not model memory (your `prices_grounded` check passed in 25 of 25 runs), structured outputs for bookings, and a test set of real-looking requests.
- **FastAPI:** async endpoints, validation with Pydantic, auth, rate limits, logging and tracing (Langfuse).
- **Cloud (gap):** Docker and Hugging Face Spaces so far; know the basics of deploying a container on AWS, GCP or Azure.
