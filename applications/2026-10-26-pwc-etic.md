- **Consulting framing:** explain one project in client terms: the problem, what you measured, the result and the limits. Practise a 2-minute version of the Shopping Assistant for a non-technical client.
- **PwC Professional framework:** have one example each of giving or receiving feedback, working with others, and improving a process. Your teaching labs give you several.
- **Case-style questions:** "A client wants a chatbot over its policies. How would you scope it, measure it and roll it out?" Answer with RAG, an evaluation set, guardrails and a pilot.
