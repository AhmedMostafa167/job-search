## Interview prep

- **Evaluation design (core):**
  - Defining "good" per use case (support chatbot: resolution, correctness, escalation, tone).
  - Building representative task sets, then code graders vs LLM-as-a-judge, and calibrating a judge against human review.
  - Production signals: thumbs, escalations, re-contacts.
  - Turning real conversations into new test cases (the "data loop").
- **A/B testing (gap; know the basics):**
  - Randomisation unit, primary and guardrail metrics.
  - Sample size and power; don't peek; novelty effects.
  - Why your offline confidence intervals are the same idea.
- **Fintech context:** hallucinated balances or fees are a compliance risk. Ground answers in tools or data rather than model memory (your `prices_grounded` check passed in 25 of 25 runs), add refusal and hand-off rules, and protect PII.
- **Model choice:** retrieval vs prompting vs fine-tuning (your LoRA work), and cost and latency per task.
- **Your research:** be ready to explain one paper in 2 minutes, including its sample and limits.
- **Why Plata:** a bank building with AI from day one, with evaluation owned by the engineers.
