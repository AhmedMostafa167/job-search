## Technical interview prep (junior ML level)

They'll test basics, and you've taught several of these. Rehearse a 1–2 minute answer to each:

1. **Overfitting vs underfitting:** how you'd spot each (training vs validation curves) and fix it (more data, regularisation, dropout, early stopping).
2. **Train, validation and test splits:** why three sets, what leakage is, and when to use cross-validation.
3. **Metrics:** accuracy vs precision, recall and F1 (and why accuracy misleads on imbalanced data); recall@k and MRR for retrieval, which are your own numbers.
4. **Gradient descent and backpropagation:** you taught this, so use the whiteboard.
5. **CNN basics:** convolution, pooling, why there are fewer parameters than in a dense network.
6. **LoRA:** what it trains (low-rank adapters on attention weights), why it's cheap, and what you'd change today versus 2024.
7. **Data cleaning:** walk through your Shopping Assistant ingestion pipeline. It loads five file formats, checks each row against a Pydantic schema, drops invalid rows and reports why.
8. **Deploying a model:** your FastAPI service, Docker Compose, tests and monitoring.
