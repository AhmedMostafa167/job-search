# AUC Master's + Fellowship (MSc Computer Science) — plan and documents

## What it is
- The **American University in Cairo (AUC)** offers graduate fellowships that pay **tuition for up to 9 credit hours a semester** (full or partial). Most also pay a **monthly stipend**, health insurance and student fees.
- You must reapply every semester and keep a GPA of **3.2+**.
- The post you saw is AUC's **Spring 2027** cycle, open **7 October – 2 November 2026**.
- Official pages:
  - https://www.aucegypt.edu/admissions/fellowships
  - https://www.aucegypt.edu/admissions/fellowships/listings
  - https://www.aucegypt.edu/admissions/fellowships/deadlines

## The catch: Spring 2027 is out of reach. Aim for Fall 2027.
- A fellowship application needs an **admission application already on file**.
  - AUC says the admission application must be submitted "at least two weeks before the fellowship deadline", and that the fellowship system only opens **7 working days after you get a student ID**.
- **Spring 2027 admission closed on 1 October 2026.** So the 2 November deadline isn't realistic for a new applicant.
  - A Gmail draft to gradserv@aucegypt.edu is ready. It asks whether any exception is possible; send it anyway.
- **Fall 2027 (starts September 2027) is the target:**

## Program: MSc in Computer Science (thesis)
- **Not** the "Master in Computing (M Comp)": M Comp students **can't get institutional fellowships**.
- **Coursework:** 24 credit hours (3 core + 5 electives), plus a 9-credit thesis.
  - Core: Algorithms & Complexity, Distributed Systems, **Advanced Artificial Intelligence**.
  - Electives that fit you: **Natural Language Processing (CSCE 5264)**, **Computational Machine Learning (CSCE 5262)**, Data-Driven Algorithms, Pattern Analysis, Advanced Data Mining.
- **Admission:** open to CS graduates. Applicants with gaps may be admitted **provisionally** with prerequisite courses, and **provisional-with-prerequisites applicants can't get fellowships**. Your CS degree should avoid this.
- **Tuition (2026–27):** **USD 627 per credit hour** for Egyptians, about **USD 5,600 per semester** at 9 credits. This is what the fellowship covers.
- Program page: https://www.aucegypt.edu/academics/graduate-programs/computer-science

## Fellowships you can apply for (Computer Science MSc)

| Fellowship | Covers | Key conditions |
|---|---|---|
| **University Fellowship** | Tuition (9 cr) + stipend + fees + insurance | All fields; academic excellence; financial need; up to 12 h/week academic work with faculty |
| **Mohammed Bin Abdulkarim A. Allehedan Fellowship** | Tuition + stipend + fees + insurance + **book allowance + bus**; **2 years** (reviewed each semester) | Arab nationals; BSc **GPA ≥ 3.2**; **relevant scientific research background** (your 2 papers); financial need; 12 h/week academic work |
| **Laboratory Instruction Graduate Fellowship** | Tuition + stipend + fees + insurance | Science & engineering; BSc **GPA ≥ 3.2**; lab-teaching ability (your TA work); financial need; up to 24 h/week lab instruction; **no outside job allowed** |

Apply for all three in the same fellowship application. The Al Alfi STEM fellowship has no new intake.

## Your to-do list
1. **Today:** send the Gmail draft to gradserv@aucegypt.edu (fill in your institute's exact name first). Message your two recommenders.
2. **This week:** request English transcripts and a graduation certificate. Book IELTS or TOEFL for **early to mid November**.
3. **By 1 December:** finish the statement of purpose in your own words and submit the admission application with the CV, transcripts and test results. Unofficial test results are accepted for processing.
4. **22 December – 17 January:** submit the fellowship application on Banner for all three fellowships.
5. **Need your input:** your **GPA** and its scale (the fellowships want 3.2/4.0 or equivalent), the **exact institute name** on your degree, and whether you want to study **full time**. The Lab Instruction fellowship forbids outside work, and the others expect 12 hours a week on campus.

## Interview prep (if the department interviews you)
- **Why AUC and why research now:** you build systems and want to answer the research questions they raise. Thesis topic: evaluating LLM systems for Arabic.
- **Your papers:** what the LoRA fine-tuning changed and how you measured layout quality; what the quantum VAE did and its limits.
- **Your evaluation method:** labelled sets, confidence intervals, ablations (72% / 56% / 41%; 23% → 43%).
- **Theory refresh:** algorithm complexity (Big-O, NP-completeness basics), probability and linear algebra for ML, backpropagation, and transformers and attention.
