- **"Show us something you built with AI coding tools."** Open the Research Assistant demo and its repo.
  - You wrote the design: the graph, the grading and reflection steps, and the retry limit.
  - Claude Code implemented it, and you reviewed and tested it.
  - Be honest that most of the code was written by Claude from your design. That is the skill they're hiring for.

  - Tests first (104 in the Shopping Assistant), CI on every push, and you read the diff.
  - An evaluation suite for agent behaviour (12 tasks × 3 runs).
  - Measure before and after every change.
