# Asking for two recommendation letters (AUC MSc, Fall 2027)

AUC needs **at least two online letters of recommendation** from people who know your academic or professional work. After you add their names and emails in the online application, AUC emails them a link. Ask them **this week**, so they have time before you submit (target: 1 December 2026).

**Best choices, in order:**
1. **The professor who supervised your graduation project or VISIONEER paper**, or your co-author's supervisor at the university.
2. **The course professor you work under as a teaching assistant** (Compiler Theory, or the neural-networks course), who has seen your labs.
3. **Your QWorld / QUAE research supervisor** from the quantum EEG paper.

Pick the two who know you best. A detailed letter from a lecturer beats a short one from a dean.
