> MACHINE LEARNING ENGINEER – HUMANOID ROBOTICS
> Schiedam (Rotterdam area) · On-site · Full-time
> Put humanoid robots to work in the real world, hands-on with the latest robotics and AI technology.
> About HAC
> At the Humanoid Application Center (HAC) we believe humanoid robotics will become a flexible, universal automation layer that fundamentally changes how physical work gets done. Together with partner companies, we work on real-world use cases in manufacturing, logistics, agriculture and facility services, and we’re building the team and the internal software stack to make this possible. All of this happens in our own humanoid robotics lab in Schiedam, where we train and test with robots such as the AgiBot G2, UBTech Cruzr Y and Unitree G1.
> Your role
> You’ll be the ML specialist on a small, cross-disciplinary engineering team. The core challenge in humanoids is closing the gap between impressive demos and reliable deployment in real work environments. You work on the learning side of that gap: imitation learning, reinforcement learning, VLAs and world models, trained and deployed on real hardware.
> We’re a small team of around 15 people, which means short lines, lots of ownership and a direct impact on what we build.
> What you’ll do
> - Train, fine-tune and deploy models across the range, from small embedded models to foundation models, VLMs and world models
> - Build and own our learning loops: teleop data collection, dataset curation, training pipelines, evaluation and redeployment
> - Develop imitation learning and reinforcement learning policies for manipulation and locomotion, in simulation and on hardware
> - Build out our simulation environments (Isaac Sim/Lab, MuJoCo or similar) for training, evaluation and sim-to-real transfer
> - Take partner use cases from “not yet possible” to demonstrated and deployed
> What you bring
> - Hands-on experience training deep learning models yourself, not only using them via APIs
> - Solid Python and PyTorch (or JAX)
> - Experience deploying models to embedded hardware (quantization, TensorRT, ONNX or similar)
> - An MSc or PhD in ML, AI, robotics or CS, or equivalent demonstrated experience
> - The right to work in the Netherlands (see below)
> Strong pluses: RL or IL on real robots, sim-to-real, ROS 2, VLAs, world models, diffusion policies and MLOps practice.
> We care more about what you’ve built than formal years of experience. We value complementary backgrounds and encourage you to apply even if you don’t tick every box.
> What we offer
> - A lab full of state-of-the-art humanoid robots: train a model in the morning, run it on hardware in the afternoon
> - Real industrial use cases with committed partners
> - Founding-stage influence on our stack and the way we work
> - A hands-on, international team at MICS in Schiedam
> How to apply
> Apply via LinkedIn or send an email to sander@humanoidcenter.nl with your CV and a short description of the most interesting or challenging project you’ve worked on: what the problem was, what you built and what you learned. GitHub, papers or project links are very welcome.
> Since LinkedIn doesn't allow you to add extra documents, please send the projects you're most proud of to sander@humanoidcenter.nl. This is an important part of your application and weighs heavily in our selection process.
> Please note: we are unable to offer visa sponsorship or relocation support. To be considered, you must already have the right to work in the Netherlands, for example as an EU/EEA or Swiss citizen, or with a valid Dutch residence permit that allows you to work.
> Our process: after reviewing your application, we’ll invite you for a 30-minute online intro call. Next is an on-site assessment of about 1.5 hours at our lab, where you’ll get a tour, present some of your work and go through a few of our real cases with the team.
> Questions first? Reach us via sander@humanoidcenter.nl
