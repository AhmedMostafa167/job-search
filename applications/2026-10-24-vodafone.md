## Interview prep

Vodafone's graduate process is usually an online assessment (numerical, logical and personality), then an assessment day (a group exercise, a presentation, and HR and technical interviews).

1. **Your 2-minute story:** the Shopping Assistant from data to agent to evaluation, with the numbers. Then why Vodafone Data Intelligence: GenAI and agents on telecom-scale data.
2. **ML fundamentals:** bias/variance, regularisation, cross-validation, precision/recall/F1/ROC-AUC, class imbalance, feature engineering. Know a telecom example such as **churn prediction**: features from usage, billing and complaints; class imbalance; precision vs recall trade-offs.
3. **Statistics:** confidence intervals (you used 95% CIs: explain what they mean and why 127 queries gives wide ranges), hypothesis testing, A/B tests.
4. **Agents:** tool/function calling, multi-step reasoning, LangGraph state and conditional edges, memory (typed facts vs checkpointer), guardrails, how you evaluate an agent (12 tasks × 3 runs, code-graded).
5. **RAG:** chunking, embeddings, vector databases (pgvector, HNSW), hybrid search plus RRF, rerankers, measuring recall@k.
6. **A telecom GenAI use case, if asked to pitch one:** a customer-care agent in Egyptian Arabic over plan and billing data, with RAG over policy documents, tool calls to check balances, handover to a human, and evaluation on real ticket samples.
7. **Group exercise:** structure the problem, keep time, and bring quieter people in. Your TA experience helps here.
