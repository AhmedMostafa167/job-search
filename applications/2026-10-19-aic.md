## Interview prep (research-centre style: expect theory plus a technical talk about your work)

1. **Your research in 3 minutes:** VISIONEER (LoRA on Stable Diffusion: data, training, evaluation, what you'd do differently) and the EEG work (quantum VAE denoising, then LDA classification).
2. **Evaluation rigour:** why 95% confidence intervals with 127 queries, recall@1/3/10, why the reranking test used a 40-query subset, and the latency benchmark (HNSW keeps about 99.7% of exact top-10 results).
3. **Transformers:** self-attention, positional encoding, encoder vs decoder, and how LoRA adapts attention weights.
4. **Arabic NLP challenges:** rich morphology, dialects vs MSA, missing diacritics, code-switching and Arabizi, tokenisation (subword units for Arabic), and scarce labelled data.
5. **Speech basics (new to you, so learn the vocabulary):**
   - **ASR:** audio → features (MFCC / log-mel spectrograms) → acoustic model; CTC vs attention-based encoder–decoder; Whisper; scored with word error rate (WER) and character error rate (CER).
   - **TTS:** text → phonemes → acoustic model (e.g. Tacotron, FastSpeech) → vocoder (e.g. HiFi-GAN); scored with MOS.
   - **MT:** sequence-to-sequence transformers; scored with BLEU and chrF.
   - **Speech-to-speech translation:** a cascade (ASR → MT → TTS) vs a single end-to-end model.
6. **Maths and statistics:** gradient descent, backpropagation (you've taught it), probability, MLE, Bayes, bias–variance.
7. **CS basics:** data structures and their complexity, a short coding task in Python, and SQL and database design.
8. **Literature review:** how you'd survey a topic (Papers with Code, arXiv, citation chains) and summarise it. Your Research Assistant agent is a related story.
