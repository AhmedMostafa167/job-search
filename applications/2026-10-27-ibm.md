## Interview prep

- **ML basics they'll test:** bias/variance, train/validation/test splits, cross-validation, precision/recall/F1 vs accuracy on imbalanced data, regularisation, and how XGBoost differs from a random forest.
- **Your evaluation story:** 127 labelled queries, 95% confidence intervals, and why you report intervals (small test sets are noisy).
- **NLP and LLMs:** tokenisation, embeddings, fine-tuning vs RAG vs prompting, and LoRA (low-rank adapters on frozen weights).
- **Consulting questions:** explaining a model to a non-technical client, and handling unclear requirements.
- **Ask them:** the client sectors in Saudi Arabia, the visa and relocation process, and the training path (IBM certifications).
