## Before the interview

1. **n8n, the one listed tool you haven't used:** spend about an hour building one small workflow, for example a webhook that sends text to an LLM node and saves the answer to a Google Sheet. Then you can honestly say you've tried it and compare it with LangGraph: n8n is quicker for wiring business tools together, while LangGraph gives more control, state and testability for complex agents.
2. **RAG questions to rehearse with your own numbers:**
   - how you split text into chunks;
   - which embedding model you used and why;
   - why hybrid search beats vector-only search: 72% vs 41% top-10 recall on your 127 labelled queries;
   - what reranking adds: right product first went from 23% to 43%;
   - how you measure retrieval (recall@k, MRR);
   - how you limit hallucinations (grounding checks, guardrails).
3. **LangGraph questions:**
   - state, nodes and conditional edges: your research assistant's reflection loop retries once (`MAX_REFLECTION_LOOPS=1`);
   - checkpointers for memory: your Postgres checkpointer;
   - when to use an agent and when a fixed workflow is better.
4. **API questions:** FastAPI with async endpoints, Pydantic request and response models, and handling LLM rate limits and cost (your 29% fewer paid rerank calls).
5. **Raya context:** their businesses suggest likely uses such as contact-centre assistants (Raya Customer Experience), document and knowledge-base Q&A for enterprise clients (Raya IT), and Arabic-language support. Your native Arabic is a plus; mention it.
6. **Expect HR to ask** your notice period and expected salary on the first call. Decide both beforehand.
