## Interview prep

- **Statistics:** confidence intervals (why 95% CIs on 127 queries), baseline vs treatment comparisons, overfitting, train/validation/test splits, precision/recall/F1, AUC.
- **Predictive models:** walk through a churn or default-risk model end to end: features, leakage, class imbalance, metrics tied to money (cost of a false positive vs a false negative).
- **SQL:** joins, GROUP BY with HAVING, window functions (ROW_NUMBER, running totals), CTEs.
- **Data validation:** your Pydantic row-level checks, plus what you'd add for a warehouse (null rates, ranges, referential checks).
- **PySpark (gap):** DataFrames vs pandas, lazy evaluation, partitions. Theory only.
- **Agentic AI:** your LangGraph agent and how an agent could help business units (document Q&A, report drafting) safely.
