## Interview prep (read before a screening call)

1. **"Is your memory vector-based?"** The posting mentions vector-based memory. No, and you should be able to say why: each user has at most 50 facts (`MAX_FACTS_PER_USER`), so all of them fit in the prompt. Similarity search would add an embedding call and could miss a hard constraint like a budget. Vector memory pays off once facts outgrow the context window, or for recalling past episodes.
2. **Expect "why X?" for each choice in the Shopping Assistant.** Why RRF rather than weighted score fusion, why HNSW and what its parameters do, why a 95% confidence interval, and why code-graded checks plus LLM-as-a-judge.
3. **Arabic-specific questions are likely.** Know the common difficulties:
   - dialects vs MSA;
   - diacritics;
   - Arabic–English code-switching;
   - right-to-left text in OCR;
   - tokenisers that split Arabic words into many tokens (cost and latency);
   - evaluating answers in a language most judges handle less well.
4. **Gaps (speech, OCR):** give the honest answer above, then show how you would measure. That is the signal they want from a junior-to-mid engineer.
