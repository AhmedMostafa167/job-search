  - Hill International — risk roles: 10+ yrs, construction.

- **Need an IT, cyber or SOC background:**
  - PwC ETIC — Cyber Risk / GRC (some need German).
  - CIB — IT Risk Officer: 10 yrs.
  - Mashreq — Information Security Risk.
  - Egyptian Banks Company — IT Risk.

## What would raise his chances

- **Lead the CV with numbers from WE:** risks in the register, KRIs tracked, RCSAs run, incidents handled, money saved or recovered.
- **Name the frameworks he used**, such as ISO 31000, COSO or business continuity.
- **Get a short certificate** if he has none (CRMP, or an ISO 31000 course). Bupa, Müller's and Beltone list one as a plus.
- **Ask about salary in the first call.** Most of these employers are banks, fintechs and multinationals.
